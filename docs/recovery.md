# Disaster Recovery & RTO/RPO

CloudStoreX provides full workspace failover and disaster recovery capabilities based on Point-In-Time snapshots and replica promotions.

## SLAs
The system supports configurable RPO (Recovery Point Objective) and RTO (Recovery Time Objective) targets, monitored actively via Chaos Engineering drills.

## Manual & Automatic Triggers
DR plans can be triggered manually via the Operations Center or automatically by subscribing to Provider Outage events.
